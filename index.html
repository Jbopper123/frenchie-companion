Pet insurance

Flat-faced breeds can be costly to insure, and some policies exclude breathing-related conditions or treat them as pre-existing. Before buying, ask:

  • Are breathing problems (BOAS) covered, and from when?
  • What is the yearly limit and the excess?
  • Is it a lifetime policy or does cover for a condition end after a year?
  • What waiting periods apply?
